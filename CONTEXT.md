# Teach

A lifelong tutor: it models what one learner knows across general computing and other general
knowledge, grows that model from the learner's own questions and work, and teaches against it.

The authoritative vocabulary is **[GLOSSARY.md](GLOSSARY.md)**. Use its terms and its `_Avoid_` lists;
do not invent a second vocabulary in code, docs or tool descriptions.

## The product in one paragraph

Teach is conversation first. An inquiry, a chapter or a piece of work becomes concepts and links in
one durable General tracker. That tracker is shared across agents and devices, so a later session
continues from the same record instead of starting over. Books, assessment and spaced review provide
structure, but they do not replace the learner's own work: progress is anchored to real output, never
self-reported reading.

## Boundaries

- One learner and one General tracker.
- One served teaching protocol; clients load the rules rather than duplicating them.
- Capture is the current release. Grading, spaced review, books, feed and richer instruments follow
  under the full-release gate.
- Employer-specific learning is outside scope.
